Falling debris meets the same 5-mil shield on your glass roof. It protects the original factory glass, so you avoid costly replacements that disturb sensors and seals.

05 — Heat rejection

98% of infrared heat never gets in.

Ordinary tint sits inside, so the glass soaks up heat first and radiates it into the cabin. Thermoshield X reflects it on the outside surface. Cooler cabin, less A/C load, more EV range.

05 — The install

No heat gun. No cracked roof.

Traditional tint is shrunk onto glass with a heat gun, and panoramic roofs have cracked from that heat. Thermoshield X goes on with a cold, water-based install that is safe for sunroof cars and EVs like Tesla and BYD.
