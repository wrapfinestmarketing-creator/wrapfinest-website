Privacy notice

Last updated 23 November 2026

WrapFinest (Blk 11 Lot 17, WrapFinest Building, Friendship Hiway, Brgy. Calibutbut, Bacolor, Pampanga) runs this website. This notice explains what we collect when you use it and why, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

What you send us

When you send a quote request we receive your name, mobile number, email (if given), car, the services and options you chose and your message. The form is delivered to our email through FormSubmit. We use these details only to reply to your request and prepare your quote, and we keep them only as long as we need them for that and for our records.

Advertising and measurement

We use the Meta Pixel and Meta's Conversions API to learn which of our Facebook and Instagram ads bring visitors and quote requests, and to show our ads to people likely to be interested. This sets cookies in your browser and tells Meta which pages and sections of this website you viewed, the options you tried, and when you send a quote request. When you send a request, your name, mobile number and email are sent to Meta in a scrambled (hashed) form so Meta can match the request to an ad you saw; Meta does not receive them in readable form from us.

We also note which ad or link brought you to the site (for example the campaign name in the link), and include it with your quote request.

You can control ads based on your activity in your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences, and you can clear or block cookies in your browser.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. Contact us at wrapfinestpampanga@gmail.com or 0998 564 0802.
